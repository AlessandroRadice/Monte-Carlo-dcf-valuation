The base case sits 19.7% above the simulated median. Most input distributions have their long tail running against value (tax up to 35%, CapEx up to 22%, margin down to 36%), so the most likely inputs do not produce the typical outcome. WACC is the largest single driver of the spread.

The six uncertain inputs

Each iteration draws one value for every input. Revenue growth and EBITDA margin move together (correlation +0.35 through a Gaussian copula), so strong years come with strong margins.

Fixed parameters and limits

  • Base revenue €145m, D&A 13% of revenue, change in working capital 3% of incremental revenue, net debt €185m, 25.0m shares.
  • WACC is kept at least 1.5 points above terminal growth, so an extreme draw cannot produce an infinite terminal value.
  • Lumina is fictional and the ranges are judgement, not market data. The model prices only the risks it defines.
  • The Excel workbook reproduces 2,000 of the 50,000 iterations with live formulas, so its statistics differ slightly from the figures above.
  • This page re-runs the simulation with fresh random draws, so its numbers move a little each time.
